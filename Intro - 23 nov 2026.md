# DevOps / 23-11-26
## Key Principles and Practices

Automation in DevOps
Automation is a key principle in DevOps that enables teams to streamline processes and reduce manual errors, leading to more efficient workflows.

Collaboration and Communication
Collaboration among development and operations teams fosters a culture of shared responsibility and improves communication, enhancing overall efficiency.

Continuous Integration/Deployment
CI/CD practices enable rapid and reliable software delivery by automating integration and deployment processes, ensuring faster time-to-market.

Continuous Feedback Loop
Continuous feedback is crucial in DevOps, allowing teams to gather insights and make iterative improvements to products and processes.

## Key DevOps principles
> To realize the full potential of DevOps, teams should follow key DevOps principles
> DevOps is more than just development and operations teams working together. It's more than tools and practices.
